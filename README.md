# Handball IQ

## Train Your Mind. Master the Game.

Handball IQ is an AI-powered platform that helps handball players develop decision making, tactical understanding and mental performance through realistic match scenarios.

## Vision

To become the world's leading platform for developing handball intelligence.

## What makes Handball IQ different?

Instead of teaching handball theory, Handball IQ trains players to think, react and make better decisions under real match pressure.

Players improve through:

- Match Scenarios
- Decision Training
- Match Preparation
- Mental Performance
- Tactical Intelligence
- AI Coach
- Match Simulator
