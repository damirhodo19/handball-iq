## C1. Goalkeeper — Widen the Barrier Only When the Low Release Appears

- Family: `gk_close_range_spread_timing`
- Cue: Breakthrough player draws the ball from high position toward the outside hip while entering six metres

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 13:14 at 26'. A backcourt player breaks through and enters six metres under control. You have stopped your advance. The ball starts high, then separates toward his outside hip for a low side release.

**Question:** When and how do you widen the saving surface?

A. **optimal** — Widen toward the ball as the low side release becomes clear, while keeping the head and torso upright
   - Correct — the movement follows the release without abandoning the high target.
B. **good** — Remain compact if the ball returns above the shoulder before release
   - Good — the cue for the low spread has disappeared.
C. **risky** — Spread as soon as the attacker enters six metres, before the ball moves low
   - Risky — an early spread opens the high finish and lob.
D. **poor** — Settle into a low, wide stance while the ball is still high
   - Poor — you lose height before the shooter has committed low.

**Explanation:** At close range, widen the body in response to a visible low release. Keep enough height to protect against a last high finish.

**A over B:** A fits the ball moving toward the outside hip; B applies only if the ball returns high.

### HR

**Situation:** Gubite 13:14 u 26. minuti. Vanjski igrač prolazi obranu i kontrolirano ulazi na šest metara. Zaustavio si izlazak. Lopta je najprije visoko, a zatim ide prema vanjskom kuku za niski bočni izbačaj.

**Question:** Kada i kako širiš obrambenu površinu?

A. **optimal** — Raširi se prema lopti kada niski bočni izbačaj postane jasan, uz uspravnu glavu i trup
   - Točno — pokret prati izbačaj bez otvaranja gornjeg dijela gola.
B. **good** — Ostani kompaktan ako se lopta prije izbačaja vrati iznad ramena
   - Dobro — signal za nisku obranu više nije prisutan.
C. **risky** — Raširi se čim napadač uđe na šest metara, prije nego što lopta krene nisko
   - Rizično — prerano širenje otvara visoki završetak i lob.
D. **poor** — Postavi se u nizak, širok stav dok je lopta još visoko
   - Loše — gubiš visinu prije nego što se šuter odlučio za niski završetak.

**Explanation:** Na maloj udaljenosti širi tijelo kao odgovor na vidljiv niski izbačaj. Zadrži dovoljno visine za moguću kasnu promjenu prema gore.

**A over B:** A odgovara lopti koja ide prema vanjskom kuku; B vrijedi samo ako se lopta vrati visoko.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr liegt 13:14 in Minute 26 zurück. Ein Rückraumspieler bricht durch und kommt kontrolliert an den Kreis. Du hast dein Vorrücken gestoppt. Der Ball beginnt hoch und geht dann zur äußeren Hüfte für einen tiefen seitlichen Abwurf.

**Question:** Wann und wie verbreiterst du die Abwehrfläche?

A. **optimal** — Beim klaren tiefen Seitenabwurf zum Ball verbreitern und Kopf sowie Oberkörper aufrecht halten
   - Richtig — die Bewegung folgt dem Abwurf, ohne den hohen Torraum aufzugeben.
B. **good** — Kompakt bleiben, wenn der Ball vor dem Abwurf wieder über die Schulter geht
   - Gut — das Signal für die tiefe Abwehr ist verschwunden.
C. **risky** — Schon beim Eintritt an den Kreis verbreitern, bevor der Ball tief geht
   - Riskant — frühes Verbreitern öffnet hohen Abschluss und Heber.
D. **poor** — In einen tiefen, breiten Stand gehen, obwohl der Ball noch hoch ist
   - Schlecht — du verlierst Höhe, bevor der Werfer tief abgeschlossen hat.

**Explanation:** Aus kurzer Distanz den Körper auf einen sichtbaren tiefen Abwurf verbreitern. Genug Höhe für einen späten hohen Abschluss behalten.

**A over B:** A passt zum Ball an der äußeren Hüfte; B gilt nur, wenn der Ball wieder hoch geht.

## C2. Goalkeeper — Stop Advancing When the Runner Regains Shooting Control

- Family: `gk_breakaway_ball_distance`
- Cue: Runner gathers the ball close to the body at eight metres after a long dribble

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 10:11 at 21'. A player is alone through the centre. You advanced during a long dribble, but at eight metres he gathers the ball close to his body and can now shoot, take another step or lob.

**Question:** What changes when the ball returns to shooting control?

A. **optimal** — Stop the advance, set the feet and read the next ball action
   - Correct — a stable set position is needed once an immediate finish is possible.
B. **good** — Advance again only if a new touch sends the ball outside shooting control
   - Good — another long touch creates a new safe moment.
C. **risky** — Continue forward until the attacker reaches six metres
   - Risky — he can release while you are still moving.
D. **poor** — Retreat immediately to the goal line when he gathers the ball
   - Poor — you return the full shooting angle to the attacker.

**Explanation:** Ball distance determines when advancing is safe. Gain space while the ball cannot be released, then become stable as soon as it can.

**A over B:** A matches the current controlled ball; B requires a new long touch that has not happened.

### HR

**Question:** Što radiš kada napadač ponovno uspostavi kontrolu za šut?

A. **optimal** — Zaustavi izlazak, postavi stopala i čitaj sljedeći pokret lopte
   - Točno — čim je završetak moguć, treba ti stabilan položaj.
B. **good** — Ponovno izađi samo ako novi dodir udalji loptu izvan kontrole za šut
   - Dobro — novi dugi dodir ponovno stvara siguran trenutak za izlazak.
C. **risky** — Nastavi izlaziti dok napadač ne stigne na šest metara
   - Rizično — može pucati dok si još u kretanju.
D. **poor** — Odmah se povuci na gol-liniju kada primi loptu uz tijelo
   - Loše — napadaču vraćaš puni kut šuta.

**Explanation:** Udaljenost lopte od napadača određuje kada je izlazak siguran. Osvoji prostor dok ne može pucati, a zaustavi se čim završetak postane moguć.

**A over B:** A odgovara trenutačno kontroliranoj lopti; B zahtijeva novi dugi dodir koji se još nije dogodio.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr liegt 10:11 in Minute 21 zurück. Ein Spieler läuft allein durch die Mitte. Während eines langen Dribblings bist du vorgerückt, doch bei acht Metern nimmt er den Ball körpernah auf und kann nun werfen, weitergehen oder heben.

**Question:** Was ändert sich, sobald der Ball wieder wurfbereit ist?

A. **optimal** — Das Vorrücken stoppen, die Füße setzen und die nächste Ballaktion lesen
   - Richtig — sobald ein direkter Abschluss möglich ist, brauchst du einen stabilen Stand.
B. **good** — Nur erneut vorrücken, wenn ein neuer Kontakt den Ball aus der Wurfkontrolle bringt
   - Gut — ein weiterer langer Kontakt schafft erneut einen sicheren Moment.
C. **risky** — Weiter vorrücken, bis der Angreifer den Kreis erreicht
   - Riskant — er kann werfen, während du noch in Bewegung bist.
D. **poor** — Bei der Ballaufnahme sofort auf die Torlinie zurückgehen
   - Schlecht — du gibst dem Angreifer den vollen Wurfwinkel zurück.

**Explanation:** Der Ballabstand bestimmt, wann Vorrücken sicher ist. Raum gewinnen, solange kein Wurf möglich ist, und stehen, sobald der Abschluss möglich wird.

**A over B:** A passt zum aktuell kontrollierten Ball; B setzt einen neuen langen Kontakt voraus.

## C3. Goalkeeper — Protect the Lob Only After Its Release Cue

- Family: `gk_breakaway_lob_trigger`
- Cue: Runner slows at six and a half metres and places the hand under the ball while the throwing motion rises

### EN

**Situation:** Tied 22:22 at 44'. You have reduced the angle against a central breakaway and are set. At six and a half metres the runner slows, brings the hand under the ball and begins an upward release path.

**Question:** How do you respond to the visible lob cue?

A. **optimal** — Take one short recovery step, stay tall and track the ball through release
   - Correct — you protect space above without losing sight or balance.
B. **good** — Hold the set point if the hand returns behind the ball for a direct shot
   - Good — the lob cue has then been cancelled.
C. **risky** — Retreat several steps as soon as the attacker slows down
   - Risky — slowing down alone does not prove a lob.
D. **poor** — Keep advancing after the rising lob release has already begun
   - Poor — you shorten the distance the lob needs to pass above you.

**Explanation:** A lob response should follow a combined ball-and-release cue, not a general change of speed. Recover only as much depth as you can control.

**A over B:** A fits the confirmed rising path; B applies only if that path changes back to a direct throw.

### HR

**Question:** Kako reagiraš na vidljiv signal za lob?

   - Točno — štitiš prostor iznad sebe bez gubitka pogleda i ravnoteže.
B. **good** — Zadrži postavljeni položaj ako se šaka vrati iza lopte za izravni šut
   - Dobro — signal za lob tada više ne vrijedi.
C. **risky** — Povuci se nekoliko koraka čim napadač uspori
   - Rizično — samo usporavanje ne potvrđuje lob.
D. **poor** — Nastavi izlaziti i nakon što je uzlazni izbačaj za lob već počeo
   - Loše — dodatno olakšavaš lopti da te prebaci.

**Explanation:** Na lob reagiraj tek nakon jasnog signala lopte i izbačaja, a ne samo zbog promjene brzine. Vrati se samo onoliko koliko možeš kontrolirati.

**A over B:** A odgovara potvrđenoj uzlaznoj putanji; B vrijedi samo ako se pokret vrati u izravni šut.

### DE

**Situation:** Unentschieden 22:22 in Minute 44. Gegen den zentralen Gegenstoß hast du den Winkel verkürzt und stehst stabil. Bei sechseinhalb Metern verlangsamt der Läufer, bringt die Hand unter den Ball und beginnt eine steigende Abwurfbewegung.

**Question:** Wie reagierst du auf das sichtbare Hebersignal?

   - Riskant — das Verlangsamen allein beweist keinen Heber.
D. **poor** — Weiter vorrücken, obwohl der steigende Heberabwurf bereits begonnen hat
   - Schlecht — du verkürzt den Weg, den der Heber über dich nehmen muss.

**Explanation:** Die Heberreaktion soll einem klaren Ball- und Abwurfsignal folgen, nicht nur einer Tempoänderung. Nur kontrollierbar viel Tiefe zurücknehmen.

## C4. Goalkeeper — Use the Recovering Defender to Narrow the Breakaway Lane

   - Correct — the defender owns the inside lane and you reduce the open finish.
B. **good** — Return neutral if the defender loses the inside shoulder

   - Risky — the outside finish becomes too large.
D. **poor** — Remain fixed in the centre of goal despite the side approach
   - Poor — your position does not match the remaining shot line.

**Explanation:** The goalkeeper can use real defender control to reduce the open lane. The position must change immediately if that control is lost.

**A over B:** A uses the currently secure inside shoulder; B is the fallback after that control breaks.

## C5. Goalkeeper — In Two Against One, Stay With the Ball Until the Pass Leaves

- Family: `gk_two_v_one_pass_or_shot`
- Cue: Central ball carrier is shot-ready at eight metres while left runner remains a passing option

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 24:25 at 49'. Two attackers come against you with no defender able to intervene. The ball carrier is central at eight metres and ready to shoot; a teammate runs free on the left, but the pass has not left the carrier's hand.

**Question:** What do you defend first?

A. **optimal** — Stay square to the ball carrier at controlled depth and shift only when the pass leaves
   - Correct — the immediate shooter remains the first threat.
B. **good** — Explode toward the receiver as soon as the ball clearly leaves the carrier
   - Good — possession and the shot line have then changed.
C. **risky** — Stand halfway between both attackers before the pass
   - Risky — the ball carrier receives too much visible goal.
D. **poor** — Commit fully to the free runner while the carrier still controls the ball
   - Poor — the current shooter can finish into an open goal.

**Explanation:** In an overload, defend the current ball and delay commitment to the next receiver. Movement begins with the actual pass, not with the possibility of one.

**A over B:** A fits the ball still held by the central attacker; B follows immediately after release of the pass.

## C6. Goalkeeper — In Three Against Two, Do Not Defend the Second Pass Before the First Is Complete

**Question:** How do you handle the possible second pass?

A. **optimal** — Rebuild the shot line to the right-side ball and stay able to push across if the second pass leaves
   - Correct — you cover the current shot without losing the next movement.
B. **good** — Push across immediately when the second pass leaves the hand
   - Good — the new receiver then becomes the current threat.
C. **risky** — Begin drifting left while the right attacker still controls the ball
   - Risky — the right-side shot opens before the pass exists.
D. **poor** — Stay on the left runner after the first pass reaches the right
   - Poor — you abandon the player who now owns the ball.

**Explanation:** Each pass creates a new ball-to-goal line. Rebuild to the current receiver first, but finish in a stance that allows the next push.

**A over B:** A is required while the right attacker controls the ball; B begins only when the second pass is released.

## C7. Goalkeeper — Use a Repeatable Legal Set Point for Seven Metres

- Family: `gk_7m_set_point`
- Cue: Thrower is ready and goalkeeper needs a stable reference behind the four-metre restraining line

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 8:9 at 17' and face a seven-metre throw. The thrower is ready behind the line, but the whistle has not sounded. You need a stable starting point without crossing the goalkeeper's four-metre restraining line before release.

**Question:** How do you establish the set point?

A. **optimal** — Settle at your trained legal depth, keep at least one grounded foot on or behind four metres and balance for both sides
   - Correct — a repeatable legal reference supports timing and balance.
B. **good** — Adjust laterally within the legal position if the thrower changes the ball-to-goal line
   - Good — lateral alignment may change while the legal depth remains controlled.
C. **risky** — Change the starting depth repeatedly to make the thrower react
   - Risky — excess movement can damage your own set and timing.
D. **poor** — Cross the four-metre line before the ball leaves the hand
   - Poor — the position is illegal and can cause a retake.

**Explanation:** A consistent set point removes unnecessary variation. The goalkeeper may react freely, but must respect the four-metre restriction until the ball leaves the thrower.

**A over B:** A establishes the base position before any cue; B is a later adjustment only if the shot line changes.

### HR
