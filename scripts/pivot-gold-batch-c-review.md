## C1. Pivot — Reseal the New Defender Immediately After the Switch

- Family: `pv_reseal_after_defensive_switch`
- Cue: Screen causes switch; original pivot defender takes ball carrier and smaller half defender remains on pivot
- Structure: 6-0 / 6v6

### EN

**Situation:** Tied 20:20 at 39'. Your screen creates a defensive switch. Your original marker takes the centre back, while a smaller half defender is left on you at six metres. He is still turning to find your body and has not established front position.

**Question:** How do you use the mismatch before it settles?

A. **optimal** — Reseal the new defender on his turning hip and show the feed before he squares
   - Correct — the switch creates value only if the reseal is immediate.
B. **good** — Clear away if the centre back beats your original marker directly
   - Good while the drive remains live.
C. **risky** — Watch the ball and wait for the new defender to set
   - Risky — the mismatch becomes normal coverage.
D. **poor** — Push the smaller defender into the goal area
   - Poor — illegal force wastes the mismatch.

**Explanation:** After a switch, read the new body immediately. Reseal the turning hip before the new marker establishes balance; otherwise the defence has repaired itself.

**A over B:** A attacks the unsettled mismatch; B applies only if the ball carrier already wins the drive.

### HR

**Situation:** Neriješeno je 20:20 u 39. minuti. Tvoj blok izaziva obrambeno preuzimanje. Tvoj prvi čuvar preuzima srednjeg vanjskog, a manji polubranitelj ostaje na tebi na šest metara. Još se okreće pronaći tvoje tijelo i nije zauzeo položaj ispred.

**Question:** Kako koristiš nesrazmjer prije nego se postavi?

A. **optimal** — Ponovno zatvori novog braniča na kuku oko kojeg se okreće i pokaži loptu prije nego se postavi ravno
   - Točno — preuzimanje stvara vrijednost samo uz trenutačno novo zatvaranje.
B. **good** — Makni se ako srednji vanjski izravno prođe tvog prvog čuvara
   - Dobro dok je prodor živ.
C. **risky** — Gledaj loptu i čekaj da se novi branič postavi
   - Rizično — nesrazmjer postaje normalno pokrivanje.
D. **poor** — Gurni manjeg braniča u vratarev prostor
   - Loše — nedopuštena sila baca nesrazmjer.

**Explanation:** Nakon preuzimanja odmah čitaj novo tijelo. Ponovno zatvori kuk oko kojeg se novi čuvar okreće prije nego uspostavi ravnotežu; inače se obrana popravila.

**A over B:** A napada nesređen nesrazmjer; B vrijedi samo ako igrač s loptom već dobiva prodor.

### DE

**Situation:** Unentschieden 20:20 in Minute 39. Deine Sperre erzeugt einen Abwehrwechsel. Dein erster Verteidiger übernimmt den Rückraum Mitte, ein kleinerer Halbverteidiger bleibt bei dir am Kreis. Er dreht noch zu deinem Körper und hat keine Frontposition.

**Question:** Wie nutzt du das Missverhältnis, bevor es sich ordnet?

A. **optimal** — Den neuen Verteidiger an seiner Drehhüfte neu sperren und vor seiner Frontstellung das Anspiel zeigen
   - Richtig — der Wechsel schafft nur bei sofortiger neuer Sperre Wert.
B. **good** — Wegbleiben, wenn der Rückraum deinen ersten Verteidiger direkt schlägt
   - Gut solange der Durchbruch lebt.
C. **risky** — Den Ball beobachten und warten, bis der neue Verteidiger steht
   - Riskant — das Missverhältnis wird zur normalen Deckung.
D. **poor** — Den kleineren Verteidiger in den Torraum schieben
   - Schlecht — illegale Kraft verschwendet den Vorteil.

**Explanation:** Nach dem Wechsel sofort den neuen Körper lesen. Die Drehhüfte sperren, bevor der neue Verteidiger Balance findet; sonst ist die Abwehr repariert.

**A over B:** A greift das ungeordnete Missverhältnis an; B gilt nur bei bereits gewonnenem Durchbruch.

## C2. Pivot — Move on the Reversal Before the Help Defender Sees You

- Family: `pv_reverse_ball_move_before_help`
- Cue: Far help defender turns head to reversing pass; path behind his back opens during flight only
- Structure: 6-0 / 6v6

### EN

**Situation:** Tied 21:21 at 42'. The ball reverses from right to left. The far help defender turns his head and shoulders to follow the pass, briefly exposing the path behind his back. Once the left back catches, he will see both ball and pivot again.

**Question:** When do you cross behind the help defender?

A. **optimal** — Move behind him during the pass and arrive with a target as the left back catches
   - Correct — ball flight hides the movement and prepares the feed.
B. **good** — Stay if he keeps chest and vision on you through the reversal
   - Good against maintained vision; here he turns away.
C. **risky** — Start after the left back catches and the defender sees you
   - Risky — the defender can hand you over cleanly.
D. **poor** — Run into his back while he watches the pass
   - Poor — use the space behind him, not illegal contact.

**Explanation:** Ball reversal creates a blind beat. Move through the space behind the helper while the pass travels and arrive as a ready target, not as late visible movement.

**A over B:** A uses the live blind moment; B is correct only if the defender never loses vision.

### HR

**Situation:** Neriješeno je 21:21 u 42. minuti. Lopta mijenja stranu zdesna ulijevo. Dalji pomagač okreće glavu i ramena za dodavanjem te nakratko otvara put iza leđa. Kad lijevi vanjski primi, ponovno će vidjeti i loptu i pivota.

**Question:** Kada prolaziš iza pomagača?

A. **optimal** — Kreni iza njega dok lopta putuje i stigni s vidljivom metom na prijem lijevog vanjskog
   - Točno — let lopte skriva kretanje i priprema dodavanje.
B. **good** — Ostani ako tijekom promjene strane zadrži prsa i pogled na tebi
   - Dobro uz zadržan pogled; ovdje se okreće.
C. **risky** — Kreni nakon prijema lijevog vanjskog kad te branič već vidi
   - Rizično — branič te može čisto predati.
D. **poor** — Utrči mu u leđa dok prati dodavanje
   - Loše — koristi prostor iza njega, ne nedopušten kontakt.

**Explanation:** Promjena strane stvara slijepi trenutak. Prođi prostorom iza pomagača dok lopta putuje i stigni kao spremna meta, ne kao kasno vidljivo kretanje.

**A over B:** A koristi živi slijepi trenutak; B vrijedi samo ako branič nikad ne izgubi pogled.

### DE

**Situation:** Unentschieden 21:21 in Minute 42. Der Ball wird von rechts nach links verlagert. Der ferne Helfer dreht Kopf und Schultern zum Pass und öffnet kurz den Weg hinter seinem Rücken. Nach dem Fang des linken Rückraums sieht er Ball und Kreis wieder.

**Question:** Wann kreuzt du hinter dem Helfer?

A. **optimal** — Während des Passes hinter ihm laufen und beim Fang mit sichtbarem Ziel ankommen
   - Richtig — der Ballflug verdeckt die Bewegung und bereitet das Anspiel vor.
B. **good** — Bleiben, wenn er Brust und Blick während der Verlagerung bei dir hält
   - Gut bei bleibender Sicht; hier dreht er weg.
C. **risky** — Erst nach dem Fang starten, wenn der Verteidiger dich sieht
   - Riskant — der Verteidiger kann dich sauber übergeben.
D. **poor** — In seinen Rücken laufen, während er den Pass sieht
   - Schlecht — den Raum hinter ihm nutzen, nicht illegalen Kontakt.

**Explanation:** Die Verlagerung schafft einen blinden Moment. Während des Passes hinter dem Helfer laufen und als fertiges Ziel ankommen, nicht spät sichtbar starten.

**A over B:** A nutzt den blinden Moment; B gilt nur bei durchgehender Sicht.

## C3. Pivot — Trail the First Wave Through the Middle, Not Beside the Ball

- Family: `pv_first_wave_middle_trailer`
- Cue: Wing carries wide; centre lane behind first runners is empty; pivot trails by three steps
- Structure: Fast Break / transition

### EN

**Situation:** You lead 6:5 at 13' on a fast break. The right wing carries wide and the centre runner is ahead. You trail by three steps. The middle lane behind the first runners is empty; sprinting beside the ball would remove width and bring your defender to the carrier.

**Question:** Which lane and depth should you run?

A. **optimal** — Sprint the middle as a staggered trailer, arriving near six after the first decision
   - Correct — you become the second-wave target without crowding the first.
B. **good** — Stop at nine if the first wave slows into set attack
   - Good transition into structure; the break is still live.
C. **risky** — Run beside the wing carrier and ask for a short pass
   - Risky — two attackers occupy one wide lane.
D. **poor** — Jog behind the play because pivots are not first-wave players
   - Poor — you lose the free central second wave.

**Explanation:** The pivot trails centrally at a different depth. That lane preserves width and arrives as the defence reacts to the first wave.

**A over B:** A supports the live break; B is the reset only after it slows.

### HR

**Situation:** Vodite 6:5 u 13. minuti u protunapadu. Desno krilo nosi loptu široko, a središnji trkač je naprijed. Kasniš tri koraka. Srednji koridor iza prvih trkača je prazan; sprint uz loptu uklonio bi širinu i doveo tvog braniča nositelju.

**Question:** Koji koridor i dubinu trčiš?

A. **optimal** — Sprintaj sredinom kao trkač iz drugog plana i stigni blizu šest nakon prve odluke
   - Točno — postaješ meta drugog vala bez gužve u prvom.
B. **good** — Zaustavi se na devet ako prvi val uspori u postavljeni napad
   - Dobar prijelaz u strukturu; protunapad je još živ.
C. **risky** — Trči uz krilo s loptom i traži kratko dodavanje
   - Rizično — dvojica napadača zauzimaju jedan široki koridor.
D. **poor** — Trči lagano iza akcije jer pivoti nisu igrači prvog vala
   - Loše — gubiš slobodan središnji drugi val.

**Explanation:** Pivot prati središnje u drugoj dubini. Taj koridor čuva širinu i stiže kad obrana reagira na prvi val.

**A over B:** A podržava živi protunapad; B je postavljanje tek nakon usporavanja.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr führt 6:5 in Minute 13 im Gegenstoß. Der Rechtsaußen trägt breit, der zentrale Läufer ist vorne. Du liegst drei Schritte zurück. Die Mittellinie hinter der ersten Welle ist frei; neben dem Ball zu sprinten nimmt Breite und bringt deinen Verteidiger zum Ballführer.

**Question:** Welche Spur und Tiefe läufst du?

A. **optimal** — Zentral gestaffelt nachlaufen und nach der ersten Entscheidung am Kreis ankommen
   - Richtig — du wirst zum Ziel der zweiten Welle ohne die erste zu verstopfen.
B. **good** — Bei Übergang in den Positionsangriff auf neun Meter stoppen
   - Guter Übergang in Struktur; der Gegenstoß lebt noch.
C. **risky** — Neben dem ballführenden Außen laufen und den kurzen Pass fordern
   - Riskant — zwei Angreifer besetzen eine breite Spur.
D. **poor** — Hinterhertraben, weil Kreisläufer nicht zur ersten Welle gehören
   - Schlecht — du verlierst die freie zentrale zweite Welle.

**Explanation:** Der Kreis läuft zentral in anderer Tiefe nach. Diese Spur erhält Breite und kommt an, wenn die Abwehr auf die erste Welle reagiert.

**A over B:** A unterstützt den laufenden Gegenstoß; B folgt erst nach Verlangsamung.

## C4. Pivot — In the Second Wave, Run the Goal-Area Line Before Posting Up

- Family: `pv_second_wave_rim_run`
- Cue: Defence recovers through centre; weak-side six-metre line open behind last defender
- Structure: Fast Break / transition

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 14:15 at 29' in the second wave. Three defenders recover through the centre and watch the ball. The weak-side goal-area line is open behind the last defender, who has not yet turned toward his goal. Stopping at nine metres would let the 6:0 form.

**Question:** What is your second-wave route?

A. **optimal** — Run the weak-side goal-area line behind the last defender, then seal when he turns
   - Correct — depth is won before the set defence exists.
B. **good** — Stop and reseal centrally only if the last defender reaches the rim first
   - Good if the rim is lost; it is open now.
C. **risky** — Post at nine metres and wait for all backs
   - Risky — the defence forms around you.
D. **poor** — Follow the ball to the strong-side wing
   - Poor — the free weak-side depth disappears.

**Explanation:** Second-wave pivot play is a race for the goal-area line. Run behind the last recovery body, then establish the seal as he turns.

**A over B:** A uses the currently open rim; B is the correction only if the defender wins it first.

### HR

**Situation:** Gubite 14:15 u 29. minuti u drugom valu. Trojica braniča vraćaju se kroz sredinu i gledaju loptu. Linija šest metara na slaboj strani otvorena je iza zadnjeg braniča, koji se još nije okrenuo prema svom golu. Zaustavljanje na devet omogućilo bi postavljanje 6:0.

**Question:** Koja je tvoja putanja u drugom valu?

   - Točno — dubina se dobiva prije nastanka postavljene obrane.
B. **good** — Zaustavi se i ponovno zatvori u sredini samo ako zadnji branič prvi stigne na liniju
   - Dobro ako je linija izgubljena; sada je otvorena.
C. **risky** — Postavi se na devet metara i čekaj sve vanjske
   - Rizično — obrana se postavlja oko tebe.
D. **poor** — Prati loptu prema krilu na jakoj strani
   - Loše — slobodna dubina na slaboj strani nestaje.

**Explanation:** Igra pivota u drugom valu je utrka za liniju šest metara. Trči iza zadnjeg povratnog braniča pa postavi zatvaranje dok se okreće.

**A over B:** A koristi trenutačno otvorenu liniju; B je korekcija samo ako je branič osvoji prvi.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr liegt 14:15 in Minute 29 in der zweiten Welle zurück. Drei Verteidiger laufen zentral zurück und sehen den Ball. Die ballferne Torraumlinie ist hinter dem letzten Verteidiger offen; er hat noch nicht zum Tor gedreht. Ein Stopp bei neun Metern lässt die 6:0 stehen.

   - Gut bei verlorener Linie; jetzt ist sie offen.

   - Riskant — die Abwehr formiert sich um dich.

   - Schlecht — die freie ballferne Tiefe verschwindet.

**Explanation:** Kreisspiel in der zweiten Welle ist ein Rennen um die Torraumlinie. Hinter dem letzten Rückläufer laufen und bei seiner Drehung sperren.

## C5. Pivot — In Recovery, Match the Opposing Pivot Before the Ball Arrives

- Family: `pv_transition_match_opposing_pivot`
- Cue: Opposing pivot reaches centre six; own middles still returning; ball carrier has inside passing angle
- Structure: Fast Break / transition

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 17:18 at 34' after losing the ball. The opposing pivot reaches the centre of six metres before your middle defenders. Their ball carrier crosses halfway with an open inside passing arm. You are the first central recovery player.

**Question:** What do you secure first?

A. **optimal** — Get goal-side of the opposing pivot, establish legal contact and call that you own him
   - Correct — the direct inside pass disappears before the ball can arrive.
B. **good** — Release him only after a returning middle clearly takes over
   - Good handover rule; no middle is there yet.
C. **risky** — Run at the ball carrier and leave the pivot behind
   - Risky — one inside pass beats the whole recovery.
D. **poor** — Point at the pivot and wait for someone else
   - Poor — you are the first player able to own him.

**Explanation:** The first central recovery body owns the opposing pivot until a clear handover. Take goal-side position before chasing the ball.

**A over B:** A removes the immediate inside goal; B is the release rule after support arrives.

   - Točno — izravno dodavanje unutra nestaje prije dolaska lopte.
B. **good** — Pusti ga tek nakon što srednji branič koji se vraća jasno preuzme
   - Dobro pravilo predaje; srednji još nije stigao.
C. **risky** — Izađi na igrača s loptom i ostavi pivota iza sebe
   - Rizično — jedno dodavanje unutra pobjeđuje cijeli povratak.
D. **poor** — Pokaži na pivota i čekaj nekog drugog
   - Loše — ti si prvi igrač koji ga može preuzeti.

**Explanation:** Prvo tijelo koje se vraća kroz sredinu kontrolira protivničkog pivota do jasne predaje. Zauzmi položaj prema svom golu prije lova na loptu.

**A over B:** A uklanja trenutačan pogodak iznutra; B je pravilo puštanja nakon dolaska pomoći.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr liegt 17:18 in Minute 34 nach Ballverlust zurück. Der gegnerische Kreis erreicht die Mitte am Torraum vor euren Mittelblockspielern. Der Ballführer überquert die Mittellinie mit offenem Innenpassarm. Du bist der erste zentrale Rückläufer.

**Question:** Was sicherst du zuerst?

   - Richtig — das direkte Innenanspiel verschwindet vor dem Ball.

   - Gute Übergaberegel; noch ist kein Mittelblock da.

   - Riskant — ein Innenpass schlägt den gesamten Rückzug.
D. **poor** — Auf den Kreis zeigen und auf jemand anderen warten
   - Schlecht — du bist der erste Spieler, der ihn übernehmen kann.

**Explanation:** Der erste zentrale Rückläufer besitzt den gegnerischen Kreis bis zur klaren Übergabe. Torseitige Position vor Balljagd.

**A over B:** A entfernt das sofortige Innentor; B regelt das Lösen nach Unterstützung.

## C6. Pivot — Counterpress Only When a Teammate Owns the Middle

- Family: `pv_counterpress_only_with_cover`
- Cue: Loose ball near pivot; centre back behind has clearly taken middle lane; outlet receiver faces own goal
- Structure: Fast Break / transition

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 25:26 at 51'. A loose ball rolls two metres from you after the turnover. Your centre back is already goal-side and calls that he owns the middle. The opponent receiving the outlet faces his own goal and needs one touch to turn.

**Question:** Is this a counterpress moment?

A. **optimal** — Attack the loose ball or receiver for one beat, then recover if he secures and turns
   - Correct — cover owns the middle and the receiver cannot play forward yet.
B. **good** — Recover immediately if the centre back loses the middle call
   - Good safety trigger; the call is currently clear.
C. **risky** — Continue pressing after the receiver turns forward
   - Risky — the press window has ended.
D. **poor** — Counterpress every turnover regardless of middle cover
   - Poor — uncovered counterpress gives away direct central breaks.

**Explanation:** Counterpress is conditional: nearby ball, closed receiver and confirmed middle cover. Press for one beat; once the opponent can play forward, recover.

**A over B:** A uses all three live conditions; B becomes correct only if middle cover disappears.

### HR

**Situation:** Gubite 25:26 u 51. minuti. Nakon izgubljene lopte slobodna lopta kotrlja se dva metra od tebe. Tvoj srednji vanjski već je prema svom golu i javlja da drži sredinu. Protivnik koji prima izlaz okrenut je prema svom golu i treba dodir za okret.

**Question:** Je li ovo trenutak za protupresing?

A. **optimal** — Napadni slobodnu loptu ili primatelja jedan trenutak pa se vrati ako je osigura i okrene se
   - Točno — pokriće drži sredinu, a primatelj još ne može igrati naprijed.
B. **good** — Odmah se vrati ako srednji vanjski izgubi kontrolu sredine
   - Dobar sigurnosni signal; poziv je sada jasan.
C. **risky** — Nastavi pritiskati nakon što se primatelj okrene naprijed
   - Rizično — prozor za presing je završio.
D. **poor** — Idi u protupresing nakon svake izgubljene lopte bez obzira na sredinu
   - Loše — protupresing bez pokrića poklanja izravne prodore sredinom.

**Explanation:** Protupresing je uvjetovan: bliska lopta, zatvoren primatelj i potvrđeno pokriće sredine. Pritisni jedan trenutak; čim protivnik može igrati naprijed, vrati se.

**A over B:** A koristi sva tri živa uvjeta; B postaje ispravan tek ako pokriće sredine nestane.

### DE

**Situation:** Ihr liegt 25:26 in Minute 51 zurück. Nach Ballverlust rollt ein freier Ball zwei Meter von dir. Euer Rückraum Mitte steht bereits torseitig und ruft, dass er die Mitte besitzt. Der gegnerische Empfänger schaut zum eigenen Tor und braucht eine Berührung zum Drehen.

**Question:** Ist das ein Gegenpressing-Moment?

A. **optimal** — Den freien Ball oder Empfänger für einen Moment angreifen und bei Sicherung und Drehung zurückfallen
   - Richtig — die Mitte ist gedeckt und der Empfänger kann noch nicht vorwärts spielen.
B. **good** — Sofort zurückfallen, wenn der Rückraum die Mittelsicherung verliert
   - Guter Sicherheitsauslöser; der Ruf ist aktuell klar.
C. **risky** — Nach der Vorwärtsdrehung weiterpressen
   - Riskant — das Pressingfenster ist geschlossen.
D. **poor** — Nach jedem Ballverlust unabhängig von Mittelsicherung gegenpressen
   - Schlecht — Gegenpressing ohne Sicherung schenkt zentrale Gegenstöße.

**Explanation:** Gegenpressing ist bedingt: naher Ball, geschlossener Empfänger und bestätigte Mittelsicherung. Einen Moment pressen; sobald Vorwärtsspiel möglich ist, zurückfallen.

**A over B:** A nutzt alle drei Bedingungen; B wird erst bei verlorener Mittelsicherung richtig.

## C7. Pivot — Hand the Opposing Pivot to the Half Before Stepping Out

- Family: `pv_def_handover_with_half`
- Cue: Back beats half’s outside shoulder; opposing pivot sits on central inside hip; half can recover inside with call
- Structure: 6-0 / 6v6

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 13:14 at 27' in central 6:0 defence. Their left back beats your half defender's outside shoulder. The opposing pivot sits on your inside hip. Your half defender can recover inside to him, but no handover has been called yet.

**Question:** What must happen before you help on the drive?

A. **optimal** — Call the pivot handover, feel the half take contact, then step to the ball carrier
   - Correct — the drive and inside feed cannot both remain open.
B. **good** — Stay on the pivot if the half cannot recover inside
   - Good — a late unsupported step would free six metres.
C. **risky** — Leave the pivot silently and attack the ball
   - Risky — the direct feed opens behind you.
D. **poor** — Hold the pivot with both arms while stepping out
   - Poor — illegal holding is not a handover.

**Explanation:** Central help begins with a handover. Transfer contact and voice responsibility to the half, then step to the beaten back; if the transfer fails, keep six metres.

**A over B:** A solves both threats through a confirmed handover; B is the safety choice when that handover is unavailable.

### HR

**Situation:** Gubite 13:14 u 27. minuti u sredini obrane 6:0. Njihov lijevi vanjski prolazi vanjsko rame tvog polubranitelja. Protivnički pivot je na tvom unutarnjem kuku. Tvoj polubranitelj može se vratiti unutra na njega, ali predaja još nije pozvana.

**Question:** Što se mora dogoditi prije tvoje pomoći na prodoru?

A. **optimal** — Pozovi predaju pivota, osjeti da polubranitelj preuzima kontakt pa izađi na igrača s loptom
   - Točno — prodor i dodavanje unutra ne ostaju istodobno otvoreni.
B. **good** — Ostani na pivotu ako se polubranitelj ne može vratiti unutra
   - Dobro — kasni izlazak bez podrške oslobodio bi šest metara.
