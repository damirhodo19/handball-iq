## A1. Goalkeeper — Find the Ball Beside the Block Without Leaving the Goal Centre

- Family: `gk_9m_near_hip_around_block`
- Cue: Shooter lowers the release beside the outside shoulder of a set block

### EN

**Situation:** Tied 10:10 at 20'. The right back prepares a lower release beside the outside shoulder of your set block. From your current stance the defender's shoulder briefly hides the ball, but the shot line remains central.

**Question:** How do you recover the sight line?

A. **optimal** — Make a small stance and head adjustment toward the visible side of the ball, then set
   - Correct — you see the ball without opening a large part of the goal.
B. **good** — Stay central if the block shifts and restores the view by itself
   - Good — no extra movement is needed once the ball is visible.
C. **risky** — Take a full step outside the block before release
   - Risky — the opposite side of the goal becomes too large.
D. **poor** — Remain fully hidden behind the block and react only when the ball is already close
   - Poor — the first visible information arrives too late.

**Explanation:** Recover sight with the smallest useful adjustment and regain balance before the release.

**A over B:** A is needed while the ball is hidden; B is better if the block restores the view.

### HR

**Situation:** Neriješeno je 10:10 u 20. minuti. Desni vanjski priprema niži izbačaj uz vanjsko rame postavljenog bloka. Braničevo rame nakratko ti skriva loptu, ali linija šuta ostaje u sredini.

**Question:** Kako ponovno pronalaziš loptu?

A. **optimal** — Malo prilagodi stav i položaj glave prema strani na kojoj vidiš loptu, zatim se zaustavi
   - Točno — vidiš loptu bez otvaranja velikog dijela gola.
B. **good** — Ostani u sredini ako se blok pomakne i sam ti vrati pogled na loptu
   - Dobro — nema potrebe za dodatnim pomakom kada ponovno vidiš loptu.
C. **risky** — Napravi puni korak izvan bloka prije izbačaja
   - Rizično — suprotna strana gola postaje prevelika.
D. **poor** — Ostani potpuno zaklonjen iza bloka i reagiraj tek kada je lopta već blizu
   - Loše — prva vidljiva informacija stiže prekasno.

**Explanation:** Vrati pogled najmanjom potrebnom prilagodbom i ponovno uspostavi ravnotežu prije izbačaja.

**A over B:** A je potrebno dok je lopta skrivena; B je bolje ako blok sam vrati pogled.

### DE

**Situation:** Unentschieden 10:10 in Minute 20. Der rechte Rückraum bereitet einen tieferen Abwurf neben der äußeren Schulter des stehenden Blocks vor. Die Schulter verdeckt den Ball kurz, die Wurflinie bleibt jedoch zentral.

**Question:** Wie stellst du die Sichtlinie wieder her?

A. **optimal** — Stellung und Kopf leicht zur sichtbaren Ballseite anpassen und dann stehen
   - Richtig — du siehst den Ball ohne einen großen Torbereich zu öffnen.
B. **good** — Zentral bleiben, wenn der Block die Sicht selbst wieder freigibt
   - Gut — sobald der Ball sichtbar ist, ist keine weitere Bewegung nötig.
C. **risky** — Vor dem Abwurf einen ganzen Schritt neben den Block machen
   - Riskant — die Gegenseite des Tores wird zu groß.
D. **poor** — Vollständig hinter dem Block verdeckt bleiben und erst reagieren, wenn der Ball bereits nah ist
   - Schlecht — die erste sichtbare Information kommt zu spät.

**Explanation:** Die Sicht mit der kleinsten nötigen Anpassung herstellen und vor dem Abwurf wieder stabil stehen.

**A over B:** A ist nötig, solange der Ball verdeckt ist; B gilt, wenn der Block die Sicht freigibt.

## A2. Goalkeeper — Lower the Hands, Not the Whole Body, Against a Sidearm Shot

- Family: `gk_9m_low_arm_sidearm`
- Cue: Shooter drops the arm beside the hip while both upper corners remain available

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 14:15 at 29'. The centre back escapes the front defender and drops the throwing arm beside the hip. The release point is low, but the shooter can still send the ball high or low.

**Question:** How do you adapt your stance?

A. **optimal** — Lower the hands slightly while keeping the hips, chest and feet balanced
   - Correct — you cover the lower release without giving away the upper goal.
B. **good** — Return the hands higher if the shooter raises the arm before release
   - Good — follow the actual release point.
C. **risky** — Drop into a full low save before the ball leaves the hand
   - Risky — the high finish is still open.
D. **poor** — Turn sideways to make the lower body smaller
   - Poor — you reduce the area available for the save.

**Explanation:** Match hand height to the release point while keeping the central body position ready for every finish.

**A over B:** A fits the current low arm; B becomes correct only if the arm rises again.

### HR

   - Rizično — visoki završetak još je moguć.
D. **poor** — Okreni se bočno i smanji površinu tijela
   - Loše — smanjuješ površinu kojom možeš braniti.

## A4. Goalkeeper — Follow the Ball Through the Crossing Action

- Family: `gk_9m_cross_step_delayed_release`
- Cue: Two backs cross, but the first player keeps the ball one step longer before the handover

### EN

**Situation:** You trail 16:17 at 33'. The centre back and right back cross. The centre back keeps the ball for one extra step while the right back runs behind him. A shot or pass is still possible from the first player.

**Question:** What should your position follow?

A. **optimal** — Stay aligned with the current ball carrier until the handover or pass is complete
   - Correct — the runner is not yet the shooting threat.
B. **good** — Shift to the second player as soon as he actually receives the ball
   - Good — the ball threat has then changed.
C. **risky** — Follow the runner before he receives the ball
   - Risky — the first player can shoot into the space you leave.
D. **poor** — Remain on the first player even after the second player receives the ball
   - Poor — after the transfer you are positioned against the wrong shooter.

**Explanation:** In a crossing action, follow the ball rather than the most visible runner. Change position only when possession changes.

**A over B:** A is correct before the handover; B is the next action after possession changes.

### HR

**A over B:** A vrijedi prije predaje; B je sljedeća radnja nakon promjene posjeda.

### DE

   - Schlecht — nach der Übergabe stehst du gegen den falschen Werfer.

**Explanation:** Bei einer Kreuzung dem Ball folgen, nicht dem auffälligsten Läufer. Die Position erst mit dem Ballbesitz wechseln.

**A over B:** A gilt vor der Übergabe; B folgt nach dem Ballbesitzwechsel.

## A7. Goalkeeper — With a Late View, Use a Compact Save Instead of a Guess

**A over B:** A is the general save action; B is appropriate only when a safe catch is actually available.
